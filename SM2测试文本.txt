Good morning, respected judges.
Today I will present a practical, low-cost and high-participation training plan designed for the hotel’s International Talent Month.
First, we will launch a zero-cost cross-cultural sharing salon. We will invite our regular long-stay foreign guests to work as voluntary guest speakers free of charge. In each sharing session, they share firsthand information about local customs, religious and dietary taboos, as well as daily service preferences from their home countries. These face-to-face sharing sessions help frontline workers pick up real-world service know-how without spending money on external professional trainers. Short refreshments made from leftover hotel buffet food will be provided to motivate regular attendance.
Second, we will carry out a daily 10-minute mini language training corner. We will design pocket-sized phrase cards collecting high-frequency Portuguese and Arabic sentences covering room service, food ordering and guest inquiry scenarios. All reception, catering and housekeeping staff gather for short recitation practice before their daily shift. Consistent daily practice helps employees master basic communicative phrases rapidly to break down daily language barriers with overseas visitors.
Third, we will hold a real-case role-play contest. We select authentic past service complaints and guest disputes collected in our hotel as role-play scripts. Employees split into groups to alternate roles between service attendants and international tourists, practicing emergency handling and standardized on-site responses. Small practical rewards such as complimentary staff meal coupons and priority leave permission will be awarded to winning groups to fire up participation enthusiasm.
All training activities rely entirely on internal hotel resources with limited extra costs and strong practical value. Long-term implementation will greatly lift employees’ cross-cultural awareness and professional competence when serving international customers.
All in all, this well-rounded and executable training program is able to cultivate a group of qualified international service talents for sustainable development of our hotel. Thank you!
